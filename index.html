📐 What is CLV and why does it matter?
Closing Line Value (CLV) measures whether you consistently get better numbers than the market's final price. If you bet LSU -3.5 and the line closes at -5, you have +1.5 pts CLV — you got a better number than all the sharp money that followed. Positive avg CLV over 20+ bets means your model is genuinely ahead of the market, not just getting lucky. Negative CLV means you're consistently betting at worse prices — a process problem even if your record looks okay short-term.
How to track: After each game, enter the closing line in the Bet Log → Closing Line column. The CLV updates automatically.
